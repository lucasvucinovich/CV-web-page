Profile

I'm a Business Administration and Data & Analytics student at IE University in Madrid, with a semester at the University of Michigan's School of Information. My work sits at the intersection of business and engineering: I build AI-powered products end to end, from the first conversation with the people who will use them to production code, tests and deployment.

Alongside it, I've worked on a digital twin for emergency drone operations, deep-learning research on satellite imagery, and strategy consulting labs with real companies — experiences that taught me to pair analytical rigor with teamwork and clear communication.
